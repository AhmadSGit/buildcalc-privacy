Privacy Policy for BuildCalc

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

BuildCalc ("the app") is a set of construction calculators provided by Lumetek ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and the choices you have.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The app does not require an account. The values you type into the calculators and the results they produce are processed on your device and are not sent to us.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The app saves your preferences (such as language, appearance, unit systems, and favorite calculators) on your device. This information stays on your device and is removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.

4. Advertising

Where applicable, the app displays ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers, IP addresses, and interaction data.

Where required by law (e.g., EEA/UK), we ask for consent via our consent management platform. You can update your choices at any time under About → Privacy Choices within the app.

To learn how Google processes data, visit Google's Partner Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with designated third-party services (such as ad providers) under their respective privacy policies.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Because data is stored locally on your device, most privacy rights can be exercised directly by clearing app storage or adjusting privacy settings in the app.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us by email at contact.me.lumetek@gmail.com.
